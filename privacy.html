Privacy Policy

Applies to:
• OpenCap Data Exporter (Chrome Web Store item egafclljokidcoiipaiojlaamgkooffb, including the same package when installed in Microsoft Edge)

Effective date: November 25, 2026.

This page is the privacy policy for both extensions. It describes what information they process from your FieldCap session, local workbooks, and optional mail connections, and what they do not do.

Who operates them

OpenCap Data Exporter and OpenCap Slidesheet are independent, unofficial tools published by Bruno Brottes. They are not affiliated with PHX Technology, FieldCap, or Pason Systems. Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/freebrew/OpenCap_PHX_Exporter/issues.

Information the extensions process

Neither extension collects or stores your FieldCap password. While you are already logged into FieldCap in the same browser, and only when you choose to fetch or sync a job, they read job information you can already see and keep it on your computer:

  • Job identity, well geometry, directional parameters, and surveys returned by FieldCap or opened from your local Excel Slide Sheet workbook.
  • Crew and personnel records, which can include names, roles, phone numbers, and email addresses.
  • BHA equipment, inventory, serials, hours, and drilled metres.
  • Daily slide and rotate metres, activity blocks, downlinks, and pipe tally entries.
  • Daily ticket cost totals and ticket counts.
  • Optional mailbox sign-in (OpenCap Slidesheet only): if you connect Microsoft 365 / Outlook or Google / Gmail to create daily drilling report drafts, the extension handles the OAuth token for that sign-in and the draft recipients, subject, report text, and inline images you ask it to compose.
  • Optional StrapSheets sign-in (OpenCap Slidesheet only): if you choose to sign into StrapSheets and click Publish, the extension stores your StrapSheets session token locally and sends the shift snapshot you chose to publish.

Both extensions also store local settings, cached CSV text, folder handles, and (in OpenCap Slidesheet) local workbook snapshots and restore points on your device. Optional network diagnostics, if turned on, record FieldCap request addresses on *.phxtech.com locally so an endpoint change can be diagnosed. They do not record passwords or activity on other websites.

How that information is used

Information is used solely to build your CSV exports, run your local directional drilling workbook, draw proximity gauges and 3D well paths, and prepare daily report email drafts when you request them. Neither extension sells user data, uses it for advertising, profiling, or creditworthiness, or transfers it to third parties outside the services you explicitly connect.

Where it goes

  • FieldCap. Read-only requests go to FieldCap hosts you are logged into (*.phxtech.com, including fieldcap-cdn.phxtech.com), using your existing browser session.
  • Your computer. CSV exports, local workbook snapshots, restore points, and generated .eml drafts stay on your device in the folder you choose, your Downloads folder, or the browser's local extension storage.
  • Optional mail providers (OpenCap Slidesheet). Only when you connect a mailbox and ask to create a daily report draft, requests go directly from your browser to Microsoft (login.microsoftonline.com, graph.microsoft.com, outlook.office.com) or Google (oauth2.googleapis.com, gmail.googleapis.com), or to the optional local Classic Outlook helper (com.phx.outlook) installed on your Windows PC.
  • Not to the publisher. Neither extension sends job, crew, survey, cost, or email data to the author, to GitHub, or to any analytics or advertising service.

Permissions

  • Host access to *.phxtech.com — read FieldCap job data while you are logged in.
  • Host access to Microsoft / Google mail APIs (OpenCap Slidesheet) — optional OAuth sign-in and draft creation when you choose to email a daily report.
  • storage / unlimitedStorage — store cached CSVs, settings, multi-sheet workbooks, restore points, and optional sign-in tokens locally on your device.
  • downloads / downloads.open — save CSVs, .eml report drafts, or the optional Classic Outlook helper ZIP, and open a generated .eml draft in your desktop mail app.
  • activeTab / scripting — read the active FieldCap tab's Job ID and run read-only FieldCap page/OData reads inside the open FieldCap tab when needed.
  • webRequest — optional read-only endpoint diagnostics on *.phxtech.com only.
  • alarms — periodic read-only FieldCap availability checks and update badge reminders.
  • identity (OpenCap Slidesheet) — optional OAuth browser sign-in for Outlook, Gmail, or StrapSheets.
  • nativeMessaging (OpenCap Slidesheet) — optional communication with a user-installed Windows helper for Classic Outlook drafts.

Retention and your choices

Local data stays on your device until you delete the files, clear the extension's storage, disconnect a linked mailbox, or remove the extension. Removing an extension deletes its browser storage.

Sharing you do yourself

If you send a daily report email, copy a CSV or workbook, or publish a snapshot, that copy is under your control and your employer's rules. This policy does not cover what happens to files after you share them.

Children

These extensions are field operations tools. They are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will change. The current policy is always this page: https://freebrew.github.io/OpenCap_PHX_Exporter/privacy.html.
